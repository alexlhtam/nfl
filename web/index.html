Space now

Each approximately one-yard grid cell shows its distance to the nearest player whose PFF role is coverage. Lighter cells mean more distance. The scale stays fixed from 0 to 8 yards; values above 8 share the same color.

Space at a location = distance to the nearest coverage defender.

Space opening / closing

We compare the same field location with 0.5 seconds earlier. Orange means the nearest coverage defender is farther away; blue means closer. The fixed scale is −4 to +4 yards, with values outside that range capped. This layer is unavailable until there is a full half-second of history.

A receiver’s separation

The timeline follows the selected receiver’s actual distance to the nearest coverage defender. The threshold is adjustable, with 3 yards as a starting point. Shading marks above-threshold intervals; the summary measures their total and longest continuous duration. By default, a window counts only while the receiver is at or beyond the line of scrimmage; turn off “Downfield windows only” to include the backfield. The full separation curve is always shown. Durations count the time between observations, never an extra interval after the endpoint. The chart scale is fixed at 0–12 yards; higher values are capped visually, while the numeric value remains exact.

What a coach should keep in mind

  • Distance is not catch probability or a guaranteed throwing window. Approach speed, leverage, throwing lanes and several defenders can change the picture.
  • Defensive roles are PFF play labels. Rushers remain visible but do not enter the coverage-distance calculation.
  • These are observed movements. A vacated area does not establish that a receiver caused a defender to move. Target and route labels are not inferred.
  • Each play ends at its last available observation. A shorter comparison freezes and is marked ended; no future movement is generated.

Field & controls

Both teams attack to the right after normalization. Orange markers are offense, blue are defense, and white is the ball. Click an orange route runner or use the receiver menu.
